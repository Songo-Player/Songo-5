# Noted credits

## Font

- **Indie Flower** by Kimberly Geswein, used for all the handwriting.
  Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/IndieFlower-OFL.txt`).
  Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Indie+Flower
- **Noto Sans CJK JP** (fallback for CJK titles) is the copy Songo already
  ships in `res://assets/fonts`.

## Images

- `assets/louis-hansel-M_dJ_ScwaLE-unsplash.jpg`: guitar photo by Louis
  Hansel on Unsplash (https://unsplash.com/photos/M_dJ_ScwaLE), under the
  Unsplash License. Shown on polaroids when there's no album art.
- `assets/paperclip_full.png`, `assets/paperclip_half.png`,
  `assets/paperclip.svg`: paperclip. Source and license not recorded yet.

## Icons

Menu and player icons are Songo's own, from `res://assets`.

## Everything else

The graph paper background (`assets/graph_paper.gdshader`), index tabs,
pencil boxes and loops, the ruler, masking tape, the battery doodle and the
scribble visualizer are drawn in code (`assets/*.gd`, `assets/*.gdshader`).
`assets/squiggle.gdshader` is the line-boil shader from the original sketch.
